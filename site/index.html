        How it works

        NIR Markets shows the prices of the 100 largest cryptocurrencies: price, change over the hour, day and week, market cap, trading volume and a 7-day chart. Prices come from CoinGecko and refresh every minute; the Fear & Greed index comes from alternative.me.

        Also here: trending coins, top gainers, a live feed of on-chain transfers and a market heatmap. No sign-up is needed to look; signing in with Google adds a watchlist, a portfolio and price alerts.
